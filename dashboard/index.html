TRANSPARENCY / MODEL SPECIFICATION

A comparable multiple, not a made-up constant.

Implied value = TVL × clamp(category median of listed market cap / TVL, 0.05, 5) × (1 + clamp(7d change / 100, −0.5, 1)) × (0.5 + 0.5 × min(1, age days / 730)), rounded to the nearest dollar.

01 / The universe

Sealed DefiLlama protocol snapshot dated 2026-11-14; 758 protocols with at least $5M TVL. Rank all 289 records without a numeric market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol, excluding CEX and release-suffixed names. The exact version rule is /\s(V\d+(\.\d+)?|v\d+(\.\d+)?|\d+\.\d+)$/. All 40 snapshot watchlist slugs are retained.

02 / Comparable evidence

Use every numeric market cap in the snapshot for category medians, including zero. Even-sized samples average the middle pair. Categories with no listed peers inherit the global median; clamp only after computing that median. Peer counts expose sparse evidence. A peer's token economics may not resemble the candidate's.

03 / Missing observations

Missing growth becomes zero. Age means days since DefiLlama listing to 2026-11-14, not protocol launch or token age; missing age uses 0.5 maturity. Future dates clamp to zero days. Values sort descending, ties use slug order. The page independently rebuilds peers and scores from the board rows embedded in data.js.

04 / Interpretation & risk

“No token recorded” does not mean “no token”. Implied value is a comparative model, not a predicted market cap, token price or airdrop reward. TVL can be double-counted; market caps and listing metadata can be incomplete. Farm plans are conditional research checklists, not verified protocol instructions or financial advice. Capital, fees and opportunity costs can be lost.
